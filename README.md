# paralevels_rearview_millennium_ios

A simple scene-based game for iOS.

Hop from scene to scene, making choices along the way, until you reach the end.

There are no complex mechanics to learn. Each scene presents a moment, and your choices determine where you go next.

---

## About

Rearview Millennium is built around a sequence of interconnected scenes. Each choice moves the player somewhere new, gradually revealing the path through the game.

The experience is intentionally simple: **choose, move, discover.**

---

## Platform

- iOS
- SwiftUI
- C scene engine
